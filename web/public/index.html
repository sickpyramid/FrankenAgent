03Use ReaFuse by hand, or from your agent

  1. 01

    Name two apps

    Type them into Terminal A and B. App names (Spotify), websites (instagram.com) and GitHub repos (vercel/ms) all work.

  2. 02

    Throw the switch, pick one

    ReaFuse finds open-source twins, dissects both and proposes three new creatures. You choose which one grows. You get a live link, and you can close the tab.

  3. 03

    Take it home

    Every creature arrives tested, with a report of what came from which app. Preview it, read the code, or download the ZIP.
